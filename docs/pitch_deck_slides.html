05 / 07
Insights from Thai Workplace Realities

What We Learned

Three chronic operational bottlenecks paralyzing Thai host companies.

Macro Regulatory Context (MHESI CWIE)
The friction is an institutional infrastructure gap between Thai universities and employers, not student apathy. Both sides want successful co-ops, but lack a common digital protocol.

The Synthesis

Interviews with 25+ Thai HR directors and engineering leads revealed that the pain is continuous across all 3 phases of the internship: pre-hire, hiring, and supervision.

Key Takeaway: Thai companies need fraud-proof screening at the gate, legally binding commitments at offer acceptance, and 1-click frictionless tracking during supervision.
1. Unverified Resume Claims Pre-Hire Drain
Applicants claim proficient React/SQL skills and 3.5+ GPAs. Live coding tests reveal basic gaps, wasting 30+ screening hours per cycle because resumes cannot be trusted.
2. Offer Hoarding & Day-One Ghosting Hiring Risk
Students accept 3–4 confirmation letters concurrently as risk-free safety nets. When one team begins onboarding, the candidate disappears without accountability.
3. 400-Hour Retroactive Logbooks Supervision Burden
Mentors face a 400-hour thick paper binder dumped on their desk in week 16, forced to sign off blindly on unverified work while filling out multi-page manual university grading rubrics.
